Selling: what happens at the counter

The sales side is the heart of smartpos. Every screen below is designed for a busy counter with a queue behind it — big targets, few taps, no hunting through menus.

1. Quick Sale — find, tap, done

  • Product search — type a few characters and the matching products appear instantly; no scrolling through a long list.
  • Category browsing — products are grouped by your own categories with images, so a cashier can find an item visually in one tap.
  • Quantity control — increase or decrease the quantity with large + / − buttons, or type it directly — correct mistakes without leaving the cart.
  • Live cart panel — every change you make is reflected immediately in the cart and in the running total, so there is no "let me check that again".
  • Add to cart screen — a larger layout for building a considered order item by item, with a clear review of everything picked.

2. Cart, discount and payment

  • Discount — apply a whole-percentage discount from 0% to 100% on the sale. The discount amount is recalculated and shown live in the totals, so the number on screen is always the number that gets saved.
  • Payment method — record how the buyer paid: Cash, Card, Mobile Pay or Other. The choice is stored on the sale and shown on its detail screen.
  • Totals panel — subtotal, discount and grand total are always visible together, with the same styling used on the receipt.
  • Sale preview — check the whole order before you commit it: items, quantities, discount, payment method and the final amount on one screen.

3. Receipts — paper or PDF

  • 80mm thermal printing — print the receipt straight to a Bluetooth or USB thermal printer from the app, formatted for an 80mm roll.
  • PDF voucher — no printer? Generate the same receipt as a PDF and share or save it — the app embeds its own fonts, so Myanmar, Thai and Japanese text prints correctly.
  • Everything on it — shop name and address, receipt number, date and time, each item with quantity and price, subtotal, discount, total, and the payment method.

4. Sale history — every transaction, on demand

  • Year filter — jump to any year of trading in one tap.
  • Status tabs — switch between All, Sold and Order, each showing a live count so you know what you are looking at before you open it.
  • Order cards — each sale is listed with its reference, time and amount; more sales load as you scroll, so even years of history open fast.
  • Sale detail — open any past sale to see every item, quantity, price, discount, payment method and the receipt again — useful for disputes and end-of-day checks.

Products & categories: set up once, sell all day

  • Add a product in seconds — name, price, stock and an optional photo. A quick add screen exists for the common case, and a full form when you want every detail.
  • Photos — take a picture or pick one from the gallery; images are compressed on the device before upload and cached afterwards, so the catalogue stays fast even on a slow connection.
  • Variants & brands — different sizes, colours or brands of the same product are tracked separately instead of being lumped into one line.
  • Categories — create and edit your own categories; the catalogue shows them as a visual showcase, and the dashboard reports how many products have no category so you can tidy up.
  • Stock add — a dedicated screen for adding quantity to an existing product, so restocking is a two-tap job rather than a full edit.
  • Product detail — one screen with the photo, price, stock status and description, plus the actions you need (edit, restock) without leaving the page.
  • Packages & bundles — group several products into a named package with its own code, quantity and product limit — ideal for gift sets, combos and promotion bundles.

Inventory: always know what is on the shelf

  • Live stock levels — the inventory list shows what you have right now, updated the moment a sale or a restock is saved.
  • Four clear statuses — every product is marked High Stock, Mid-Cap Stock, Low Stock or Out of Stock, with colour coding, so a glance tells you what needs attention.
  • Low-stock alerts — the app raises local and push notifications when products run low, including a stock report you can open and read item by item.
  • Restock without errors — add quantity through the stock-add screen; the status recalculates automatically, so nobody has to remember to "update the spreadsheet".
  • Dashboard roll-up — in-stock and low-stock totals broken down into High / Mid / Low / Out, so you can see the shape of your stock in one number.

Purchases & suppliers: keep restocking honest

  • Supplier records — add suppliers once and reuse them; no more searching chat history for the price you paid last time.
  • Purchase orders — record what you bought, from whom, and when, using a simple add-purchase sheet.
  • Purchase items list — see every purchase with its status, and open any one for its full detail.
  • Accuracy that compounds — purchases feed your stock figures, so your inventory reflects reality instead of memory.

Dashboard & reports: the numbers, without a spreadsheet

Open the app and the first screen tells you how the shop is doing today — no export, no formulas, no waiting for someone to prepare a report.

  • Total sales — your takings shown in Kyat (MMK), with the number of sales, the average sale, and what is still in cart.
  • Products & categories — total products, categories, packages, brands and products with no category.
  • Stock health — in-stock and low-stock figures split into High / Mid / Low / Out.
  • Monthly sales chart — twelve months of sales rendered as a chart with month labels, so seasonal ups and downs are obvious.
  • Top products — which items are actually carrying the shop, by units and by amount.
  • Recent activity — the latest sales with their amounts and times, one tap from the top of the dashboard.
  • Detailed table view — a full dashboard table screen for when you want the underlying rows, not just the headline numbers.

Offline mode: the internet goes down, the selling does not

  • Local cache — your shop data lives on the device as well as in the cloud, so the app opens and the catalogue loads even with no connection.
  • Outbox queue — sales made offline are written to a queue and held safely until the connection returns.
  • Automatic retry — queued operations are retried with back-off, then synced in order — nothing is lost and nothing is duplicated.
  • Visible status — a sync status indicator in the app shows whether everything is up to date or waiting to send, so you always know where you stand.

Notifications: the shop tells you what needs you

  • Push notifications — delivered even when the app is closed, for stock reports and updates.
  • Local alerts — on-device reminders for low stock that work without any connection at all.
  • Notification centre — every alert is kept in a list you can reopen, with a detail screen for stock reports and feedback confirmations.

Languages, account and settings

  • Four languages — Myanmar, English, Thai and Japanese, switchable in Settings. The picker shows each language in its own script, so anyone can find their own.
  • Account security — registration with account verification, forgot-password and reset-password flows, a password policy that rejects weak passwords, and login tokens stored in the device's secure keystore/keychain rather than in plain text.
  • Profile & settings — manage your account details and preferences in one place.
  • Feedback — a feedback form inside the app that reaches the team directly, with a confirmation you can see afterwards.
  • Legal documents — Terms of Service and Privacy Policy are readable inside the app, not buried on a website.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need internet to use it?

No. You can keep selling with no connection at all — the sales are stored on the device and uploaded automatically as soon as you are back online.

Which devices do I need?

Any Android phone or tablet, or a computer with a modern web browser. No special hardware is required.

Can I print receipts?

Yes — to an 80mm thermal printer, or as a PDF you can save or share if you do not have a printer.

What if I enter something wrong?

Products, prices and stock can be edited at any time, and every past sale can be reopened and checked in the sale history.

What happens if I change phones?

Your data is held in the cloud as well as on the device, so signing in on a new device brings your shop back with you.
